Two posts a week. Tuesday and Thursday mornings perform best with this audience.

3Format Rules
Image size1200 × 627 landscape fills the most feed space. 1080 × 1350 portrait also works.
Post lengthUnder 1,300 characters. The first two lines are all that shows before "see more", so put the hook there.
Line breaksShort paragraphs, one to two lines each. Walls of text get skipped.
Hashtags3 maximum. More looks like noise on LinkedIn.
LinksPut them in the first comment, not the post. LinkedIn suppresses reach on posts with outbound links.
VideoUpload natively. Never post a YouTube link. Add captions since most watch on mute.
Hashtags to rotate through
#officefurniture#commercialinteriors#facilitiesmanagement #officefitout#workplacedesign#systemsfurniture #officedesign#construction
4Tone
Works
  • "42 workstations. One weekend. Zero punch list items."
  • "Most install problems are site readiness problems nobody checked."
  • "Crews available week of the 17th."
Does not
  • "Excited to share our latest project!"
  • "Passionate about transforming workspaces"
  • "Thrilled to announce"
This audience are operators. Numbers, constraints, outcomes. No adjective that cannot be verified.

  • Facility and workplace managers at GTA companies
  • Office designers and workplace strategists
  • Commercial property and building managers

The highest return habit. Comment on posts from facility managers, dealers and designers. Comments generate more profile views than posting does and take five minutes a day.
Part 6 — Scheduling and Measurement
8SchedulingNative, free
  1. Click Start a post from the profile or company page.
  2. Write the post and attach the image or video.
  3. Click the clock icon next to the Post button.
  4. Set the date and time, click Next, then Schedule.
  5. Manage scheduled posts from the same clock icon.
LinkedIn schedules up to three months ahead. No paid tool needed.
Post from Shane's profile first, then reshare to the company page a day later. Personal reach on LinkedIn is significantly higher than page reach.
9What to TrackMonthly
Analytics live under Analytics on the company page, and under Post impressions on Shane's profile.
Track these
  • Connection requests accepted
  • Replies to outreach messages
  • Profile views from furniture companies
  • Conversations started
  • First jobs booked from new contacts
Ignore these
  • Follower count
  • Likes
  • Impressions
  • Hashtag reach
The number that matters at 90 days: how many new retailer or dealer contacts produced at least one job. Target is two.
