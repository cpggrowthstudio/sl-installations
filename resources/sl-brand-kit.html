What this is: the brand standards for SL Installations, and how to set them up in Canva so every design stays consistent. Setup takes about 15 minutes and only needs doing once.
Part 1 — The Standards
1Colours
Red is for accents, headlines and calls to action. It should never be a large background except on announcement posts such as hiring or availability.
#D10000SL Red
#0D0D0DSL Black
#9A9A9AGrey
#F5F4F2Off White
#FFFFFFWhite
Note on the red. Some earlier Canva templates were built with #D40201, which is very slightly different. #D10000 is the correct value and matches the live website. Update any template still using the old value.
2FontsBoth free in Canva
Bebas Neue
Headlines and large numbers. Always uppercase.
Barlow
Body copy, sublines, captions and contact details.
Never use anything else. If a font is not one of these two, it is not on brand.
3Logos
FileUse it for
logo-horizontal.pngWebsite header and footer, LinkedIn banner, anything wide
logo.pngSquare format. Social profile pictures, favicon, Google Business Profile
logo-signature.pngEmail signatures only. Has rounded corners baked in
logo-white-bg.pngPrinting on light or white backgrounds
Never stretch, recolour or rotate the logo. Scale proportionally only. Leave clear space around it equal to the height of the SL block.
4Photography
  • Real job sites only. No stock photography, ever
  • Wide shots that show the full installed space
  • Natural light where possible
  • Clean, finished rooms. No packaging, tools or debris in frame
  • Get client permission before posting any recognisable space
5Tone of Voice
Direct and practical. Say what happened, where, and what it took. No hype, no jargon, no motivational filler. If a crew member would not say it out loud, do not write it.
Sounds like SL
  • "42 workstations. One weekend. Zero punch list items."
  • "Reconfiguration in Mississauga. Same furniture, new floor plan."
  • "Crews available week of the 17th."
Does not
  • "Transforming workspaces, empowering teams!"
  • "Excited to announce our latest project"
  • "Passionate about quality installations"
Two writing rules. No hyphens in copy, they look automated. Testimonials are never edited, they are real customer words.
Part 2 — Setting It Up in Canva
6Build the Brand Kitcanva.com/brand
Canva Pro required. On the free plan, skip this and set colours manually each time using the hex codes above.
  1. Go to canva.com/brand and open Brand Kits.
  2. Open the kit named SL Installations & Solutions Inc. or create it if it does not exist.
  3. Under Colours, click add and enter each of the five hex codes above. Name them SL Red, SL Black, Grey, Off White, White.
  4. Under Fonts, set Bebas Neue as the heading font and Barlow as the body font.
  5. Under Logos, upload all four logo files.
  6. Under Photos or Uploads, add your best finished project photos so the team is not hunting for them.
  7. Save. Every new design will now offer these as one click options.
Give the team edit access. Canva → Share → invite by email with Can edit. Otherwise they copy designs into their own accounts and the brand drifts within a month.

All templates live in Canva, already built with the brand colours and fonts. Open a template, swap the photo and text, then export. Never edit the master.
