3Panacea Surgical ClinicWeek 2
gallery/02_Panacea Surgical Clinic/01.jpg
Project ShowcaseInstagramLinkedIn
40 chairs and 5 tables for the patient lounge at Panacea Surgical Clinic in Burlington. Modular soft seating laid out so groups can sit together without anyone feeling boxed in, and a clear path through the middle for wheelchairs and walkers. Clinic waiting rooms are harder than they look. Everything has to be easy to clean, easy to move, and comfortable for someone who is nervous. Built to the floor plan, installed outside clinic hours.
#healthcaredesign #officefurniture #commercialinteriors #burlingtonontario #waitingroom
Why this one matters: it shows range. Most installers only post desks. Healthcare and soft seating opens a different type of client.

5Equiton PartnersWeek 3
gallery/03_Equiton Partners Inc./01.jpg
Project ShowcaseInstagramLinkedIn
Boardroom install for Equiton Partners. A 16 seat table does not arrive in one piece. It comes in sections that have to be levelled, aligned and joined so the seam disappears and the whole surface reads flat. Power and data run through the centre, chairs matched to the table height, and the room left ready for a meeting the next morning. The table is the first thing anyone notices in a boardroom. Worth getting right.
#boardroom #officedesign #commercialinteriors #workplacedesign #officefitout
Why the detail: explaining that a big table arrives in sections signals craft. It tells a dealer you know what you are doing.
6Allied PropertiesWeek 3
gallery/01_Allied Properties/00001.JPG
Install ProofInstagramLinkedIn
Brick and beam heritage space for Allied Properties. Original hardwood, exposed timber columns and a ceiling you cannot drill into. Older buildings rarely have a level floor, so every desk run had to be shimmed and checked as we went. Bench desking with privacy screens, laid out to work around the column grid rather than fight it. Heritage buildings take longer. They also look better when they are done.
#brickandbeam #heritagebuilding #officefitout #torontooffice #commercialinteriors
Why this performs: the space is genuinely photogenic, and naming a real constraint like an uneven floor is more credible than saying the job went well.
7Dole Foods of CanadaWeek 4
gallery/04_Dole Foods of Canada Co./01.jpg
Install ProofInstagramLinkedIn
Panel system workstations for Dole Foods of Canada. Fabric panels, wood worksurfaces and integrated storage, installed against a glass partition wall so sightlines stayed open across the floor. Panel systems are the most common thing we install and the easiest to get slightly wrong. Panels out of plumb by a few millimetres compound across a run until the last workstation does not line up. We check as we build, not at the end.
#systemsfurniture #officefurniture #workstations #commercialinteriors #officefitout
The technical line is deliberate. Anyone who has managed an install knows that compounding error is real. It signals you have done this a lot.
