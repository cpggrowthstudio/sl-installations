9Building ConnectionsOngoing
Who to target, in priority order:
  • Facility managers and office managers in Toronto and the GTA. They sign off on installs.
  • Commercial furniture dealers. They subcontract installation constantly and are your highest value connection.
  • Office designers and workplace strategists. They specify installers.
  • Commercial real estate and property managers. They handle tenant fit outs.
  • General contractors doing commercial interiors.
Search these terms in LinkedIn:
facility manager Torontooffice manager GTA commercial furniture dealer Ontarioworkplace designer Toronto tenant fit out
Connection request message:
Hi [Name], I run a commercial furniture installation company in the GTA. We handle a lot of office build outs and reconfigurations, so we likely cross paths on projects. Happy to connect.
Send 10 to 15 per day, no more. Going higher triggers LinkedIn's limits. Never pitch in the first message.
