SL Installations & Solutions
Audience: furniture retailers, dealers and the salespeople inside them who refer installation work. This document covers Instagram content and the templates. LinkedIn is covered in its own playbook.

Part 1 — The Audience
1Who We Are Talking To
Roughly 200 to 300 relevant people across the GTA: owners, showroom managers and salespeople at office furniture retailers and dealers. Referrals are made by individual salespeople, so each person is a separate opportunity.
What they care about, in order:
  • The crew shows up, on time and at full strength
  • No damage to product or the client's building
  • A short punch list, closed quickly, with someone owning it
  • Furniture installed exactly to plan
  • Their customer has a good experience and credits them for it
Every post should speak to one of those five. Content that does not is decoration.
The strongest proof available. Customers already name Shane and the crew in reviews on a retailer's own Google page. Use that in one to one conversations. Keep it general in public posts.

4Caption Formula and Examples
Same four line structure, but the hook now leads with a number or a problem solved, because that is what a dealer scans for.
HOOK — a number or a specific outcome. CONTEXT — the constraint. Timeline, occupied building, weekend only. DETAIL — what was done and how it went right. CTA — availability, or an invitation to talk.
Install proof
42 workstations. One weekend. Zero punch list items. The client needed the floor live Monday morning, so the whole install ran Saturday and Sunday. Panel systems, height adjustable desks, full cable management. Site cleared and swept before we left Sunday afternoon. If you have a job that has to be done outside business hours, that is most of what we do.
Process
Five things we confirm before a truck leaves our yard. Floors down and cured. Power and data live. Loading dock booked. Certificate of insurance filed with the building. Floor plan confirmed against what was ordered. Most install day problems are not install problems. They are site readiness problems that nobody checked. We check all five. It is why our punch lists are short.
Capacity
Crews available the week of March 17. Burlington, the GTA and Hamilton. Systems furniture, reconfigurations, decommissions. If you are short a crew or have a job outside your usual radius, we can cover it. We install under your brand, so your customer sees your company. 416.616.2817
Customer proof
"Shane at SL Installations and his team were very efficient and professional. Highly recommended." That review is not on our page. It is on the page of the company that sold the furniture. That is the point of what we do. The customer buys from you, we install it, and you get the credit. Happy to do the same for your customers.
5Tone
Works with dealers
  • "42 workstations. One weekend. Zero punch list items."
  • "Most install problems are site readiness problems nobody checked."
  • "Crews available week of the 17th."
Does not
  • "Transforming workspaces!"
  • "Excited to share our latest project"
  • "Passionate about quality installations"
Dealers are operators. Numbers, constraints and outcomes. No adjectives that cannot be verified.

Part 3 — Video
7The Highest Value Asset
One time lapse per job. Phone on a tripod in a corner before anything comes off the truck, native time lapse mode, plugged into a power bank. Costs about five minutes of crew time and produces the single most persuasive asset SL can own.
Then give it to the dealer. This is the differentiator no competitor offers:
Every job includes a time lapse you are free to use in your own marketing. Your branding, your channels. We stay invisible.
A salesperson closing a fit out can show a nervous client exactly how install day goes. That makes SL useful beyond labour, and hard to replace.
Second: site documentation as a service. Walk through video on arrival and departure. Kills damage disputes before they start. Ninety seconds per job.
We document every site on arrival and departure. If there is ever a question about damage, we have it on file.
Formats ranked:
  • Install time lapse — 30 seconds. Works everywhere, reusable by the dealer.
  • Before and after reveal — 15 seconds. Highest completion rate.
  • Shane to camera on process — 60 to 90 seconds. Low views, converts dealers.
  • Crew at work — 10 to 15 seconds. Builds the human layer.
Publish transcripts. Put the text of every process video on the website. That is what makes the content extractable by search and AI systems, and it is the step everyone skips.

Part 7 — Measuring
13What to Track
Reach and followers are the wrong metrics here. The audience is 300 people. Judge by conversations, not crowds.
Track these
  • Connection requests accepted
  • Replies to outreach messages
  • Profile views from furniture companies
  • Conversations started with dealers
  • First jobs booked from new partners
Ignore these
  • Follower count
  • Likes
  • Impressions
  • Hashtag reach
The only number that matters after 90 days: how many new retailer or dealer relationships have produced at least one job. Target is two.
