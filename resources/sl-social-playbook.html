5Caption Formula
Four lines, every time. Keeps captions consistent without needing to think about structure.
HOOK — one specific line. What, where, how many. CONTEXT — who the client was or what the space needed. DETAIL — the scope, the timeline, or the problem solved. CTA — a link, a question, or an invitation to get in touch.

6Hashtag Sets
How many to use, by platform. More is not better. Instagram now prioritises keywords in the caption over hashtags, so a small number of highly relevant tags beats a wall of them.
Instagram3 to 5 tags. Pick the most relevant from a set, do not paste all 15. Put them at the end of the caption or in the first comment.
Facebook1 to 2, or none. Hashtags do very little on Facebook. Skip them unless one is genuinely relevant.

Part 5 — The Monthly Routine
8Batching a Month of ContentAbout 2 hours
  1. Collect photos. Ask the crew to send job site photos to one shared folder. Aim for 15 to 20 a month.
  2. Pick 10 posts across the five pillars using the split in section 4.
  3. Build the graphics in Canva by duplicating the matching template.
  4. Write captions using the four line formula.
  5. Add hashtags from a rotating set.
  6. Schedule everything in Meta Business Suite for Instagram and Facebook.
  7. Log it in the content calendar so nothing repeats.
Weekly, 10 minutes: check comments and direct messages on both platforms and reply. Engagement matters more than posting volume.
9Schedulingbusiness.facebook.com
First time setup
  1. Go to business.facebook.com and log in with the Facebook account that manages the SL page.
  2. If prompted, create a Business Portfolio and name it SL Installations & Solutions.
  3. Open Settings → Accounts → Instagram accounts and connect @sl_installations_inc.
  4. Confirm the Facebook Page is connected under Accounts → Pages.
  5. Both accounts should now appear in the top left switcher.
Scheduling a post
  1. In the left menu click Planner, then Create post.
  2. Under Post to, tick Facebook and Instagram. Untick either if the post is only for one.
  3. Upload the image. For a before and after, upload both images in order to create a carousel.
  4. Paste the caption. Add hashtags at the end.
  5. Click Schedule at the bottom rather than Publish.
  6. Pick the date and time, then click Schedule again to confirm.
  7. The post now appears in the Planner calendar view. Click any scheduled post to edit or delete it.
Best times to post for this audience: weekdays between 7am and 9am, or 4pm and 6pm. Facility managers check phones before work and at end of day.
No paid tool needed. Meta Business Suite schedules up to 75 days ahead, free.

Part 7 — Reviewing Performance
12Where to Find the NumbersMonthly, 15 minutes
Instagram. Open the app, go to your profile, tap Professional dashboard then Account insights. Set the range to last 30 days.
Facebook. Meta Business Suite → Insights.
What actually matters, and what to ignore
Watch these
  • Reach — how many people saw it
  • Saves and shares — the strongest signal a post was genuinely useful
  • Profile visits — people wanting to know more
  • Website clicks — the closest thing to a lead
  • Quote requests mentioning social
Do not chase these
  • Follower count — a big local audience of the wrong people is worthless
  • Likes — easiest action, means the least
  • Impressions alone — counts repeats, not people
The monthly review, three questions
  1. Which post had the highest reach and saves? Note the pillar and the template. Do more of that next month.
  2. Which pillar underperformed? If educational posts consistently flop, replace one with a project showcase.
  3. Did anyone mention social when they got in touch? Add a line to the quote form asking how they heard about SL. That is the only real proof this is working.
Give it three months before judging. Social for a B2B installer is a slow trust build. One facility manager who has watched your work for six months and then calls is worth more than a year of likes.
