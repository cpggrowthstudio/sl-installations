Your Own Brand Kit Setup
For Taelor
Why this exists: the SL Brand Kit in Canva right now lives under the agency's account (Ahmed, CPG Growth Studio), so it can only ever be viewed and used there, not edited by anyone else, and it depends on that account staying active. This page gets you your own independent copy: same colours, fonts, logos and templates, but owned by you. About 20 minutes, one time only.
1Duplicate the Two TemplatesIn Canva
Open each template below. Use the ⋮ menu → Make a copy (or Duplicate) to save your own version into your account. If that option is greyed out, ask Ahmed to change your share permission from view to edit, then try again.

Instagram & Facebook, 1080 × 1350 SL LinkedIn Templates

2Create Your Own Brand Kitcanva.com/brand
Go to canva.com/brand and create a new Brand Kit. Name it SL Installations & Solutions Inc.
Colours. Add each of these five, named exactly as shown.
#D10000SL Red
#0D0D0DSL Black
#9A9A9AGrey
#F5F4F2Off White
#FFFFFFWhite
Fonts. Both are free in Canva, search by name.
Heading fontBebas Neue
Body fontBarlow
Never use anything else. If a font is not one of these two, it is not on brand.
3Add the LogosDownload, then upload to your kit
Right click each link and save, then upload all three under Logos in your Brand Kit.
logo-horizontal.pngWide format. Banners, headers, anything landscape
logo.pngSquare format. Profile pictures, favicon
logo-signature.pngEmail signatures only, rounded corners baked in
Never stretch, recolour or rotate the logo. Scale proportionally only.
4Done
From here, every new design offers your colours, fonts and logo as one click options, and the two duplicated templates are yours to edit freely. Nothing here depends on the agency's account anymore.
